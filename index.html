📜 SUPERDENSE CODING CODEX

What is Superdense Coding?

Superdense coding is a quantum communication protocol discovered by Charles Bennett and Stephen Wiesner (1992). It allows Alice to transmit two classical bits to Bob by sending only one physical qubit, provided they share prior quantum entanglement!

How Bob Decodes (Inverse Bell Measurement)

Once Bob receives Alice's single qubit, he has both qubits of the pair. To read the two bits, he performs an inverse Bell measurement:

  1. CNOT Gate: Uses Qubit A as control and Qubit B as target. This disentangles the system so Qubit B is strictly in $|0\rangle$ or $|1\rangle$.
  2. Hadamard Gate: Applied to Qubit A. This converts phase differences into bit values.
  3. Standard Measurement: Bob measures both qubits in the computational basis, deterministically yielding the exact two classical bits Alice selected!
